Quantum Circuit Composition

Hadamard Gates (H): Create superposition states, enabling quantum parallelism across all 25 qubits.

RY / RZ Rotation Gates: Parameterized rotations for fine-tuned quantum state preparation and optimization.

CNOT Entanglement Gates: Create quantum correlations between qubits, enabling complex multi-qubit operations essential for QAOA.
